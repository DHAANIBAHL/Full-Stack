Programs across three divisions.

Eighteen majors, all reachable from a shared first two years. Course numbers below follow the college catalog.

Sciences

Lab-based coursework with year-round field access to the Marsh Field Station.

ENV 101 Environmental Science Estuary ecology, watershed chemistry, and conservation policy, with weekly fieldwork at the marsh. B.Sc.
BIO 101 Biology Cellular and organismal biology, with a required lab sequence through the second year. B.Sc.
CSC 101 Computer Science Taught as a liberal art: systems and theory alongside a studio track in applied software. B.A.
MTH 101 Mathematics Pure and applied tracks, from analysis and topology to statistics and modeling. B.Sc.
PHY 101 Physics Mechanics through modern physics, with independent research required in the final year. B.Sc.

Humanities & social sciences

Seminar-format courses, capped at twenty students from the first year on.

ECO 101 Economics Micro and macro theory grounded in case work on regional and coastal economies. B.A.
PHI 101 Philosophy Ethics, logic, and political theory, taught through primary texts and weekly discussion. B.A.
HIS 101 History World and regional history with a required archival research project in the third year. B.A.
PSY 101 Psychology Cognitive and social psychology, with a supervised research practicum. B.A.
ENG 101 Literature Close reading and critical writing across periods, ending in an independent thesis. B.A.

Arts

Studio-based, housed in the Ellery Studio Building with its own public gallery.

ART 101 Studio Art Drawing, painting, and sculpture, culminating in a public thesis exhibition. B.A.
MUS 101 Music Performance and composition, with ensemble participation required every semester. B.A.
THR 101 Theater Acting, direction, and design, staged in the college's black-box theater each term. B.A.
